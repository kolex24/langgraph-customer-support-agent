# Khalid Electronics — Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility
A customer may qualify for a refund when:
- an eligible product was returned within the permitted return window;
- the wrong product was delivered;
- the product arrived damaged;
- the product is confirmed to be defective.

## Refund Approval
All refunds require business-owner approval.

The AI support agent may:
1. identify the customer's request;
2. retrieve the relevant policy;
3. verify the customer's order;
4. check basic eligibility;
5. create a refund approval request.

The AI support agent must not independently execute a refund.

## Refund Amount
The approved refund amount is normally based on the eligible amount associated with the returned product or order.

Delivery charges may be treated separately depending on the reason for the return and the business owner's decision.

## Processing
After approval, the refund is processed through the appropriate payment channel.

The customer should receive a notification when the refund has been approved or rejected.

## Outside the Return Window
Requests outside the 14-day return window should normally be escalated to a human for review rather than automatically approved.

## Fraud or Suspicious Activity
Suspicious, inconsistent, or potentially fraudulent requests must be escalated to a human and must not be automatically refunded.
