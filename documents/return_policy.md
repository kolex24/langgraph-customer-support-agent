# Khalid Electronics — Return Policy

## Return Window
Customers may request a return within 14 calendar days of receiving an eligible product.

## Eligible Returns
A product may be returned when:
- the wrong product was delivered;
- the product arrived damaged;
- the product is defective;
- the product is materially different from its description.

## Condition Requirements
Unless the product is defective or damaged on arrival, returned products should be unused and include the original packaging, accessories, and proof of purchase.

## Non-Returnable Situations
A return may be rejected when:
- the return window has expired;
- the product shows customer-caused damage;
- required accessories are missing;
- there is evidence of misuse;
- the product has been modified without authorization.

## Return Request
Customers should provide their order number and explain the reason for the return.

The support agent may collect information and check order details, but final refund approval remains with the business owner.

## Exchanges
An exchange may be offered when stock is available. Exchanges are subject to the same eligibility requirements as returns.

## Important
Eligibility information is not the same as refund authorization. The support agent must not promise that money will be refunded before the required approval is completed.
