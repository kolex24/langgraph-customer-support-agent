# Khalid Electronics — Discount Policy

## Automatic Discounts
The support agent may apply an approved discount of up to 5% when the customer and order meet the applicable promotional conditions.

## Human Approval Required
Any discount above 5% requires business-owner approval.

The AI agent must never apply an unauthorized discount simply because a customer requests one.

## Discount Requests
For a discount request, the agent should record:
- customer;
- order, when applicable;
- requested discount;
- reason;
- applicable promotion or policy;
- approval status.

## Maximum Discount
The standard maximum discretionary discount is 20%.

Requests above 20% should be escalated to a human and should not be automatically approved.

## Promotional Discounts
Specific campaigns may have their own terms. If a promotion is not present in the current business documents, the agent should not invent promotional rules.

## Approval
When approval is required, the agent should create a pending approval request and tell the customer that the request is awaiting business-owner review.
