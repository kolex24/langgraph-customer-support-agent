# Khalid Electronics — Shipping Policy

## Lagos Deliveries
Standard delivery within Lagos costs ₦3,000.

Estimated delivery time:
- Lagos mainland: 1–2 business days
- Lagos Island: 1–3 business days

## Outside Lagos
Standard delivery outside Lagos costs ₦5,000.

Estimated delivery time is 3–5 business days.

## Free Delivery
Orders with a value of ₦150,000 or more qualify for free standard delivery within Nigeria.

Free delivery does not guarantee a specific delivery date.

## Order Processing
Orders are normally processed within 1 business day after payment confirmation.

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays may begin processing on the next business day.

## Delivery Delays
Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Delays may occur because of weather, courier disruptions, public holidays, incorrect addresses, or other circumstances outside the company's control.

## Address Changes
Customers should contact support immediately if they need to change their delivery address. Address changes cannot be guaranteed after an order has been shipped.

## Failed Delivery
If a courier cannot complete delivery because the customer is unavailable or the address is incorrect, additional delivery arrangements may be required.

## International Delivery
Khalid Electronics currently does not offer international delivery.
