# Khalid Electronics — Warranty Policy

## Warranty Coverage
Warranty coverage depends on the product category and manufacturer terms shown on the product listing.

## General Warranty Period
Unless a product listing states otherwise, eligible electronics carry a 12-month limited manufacturer warranty.

## Warranty Does Not Normally Cover
Warranty service does not normally cover:
- accidental damage;
- liquid damage;
- misuse;
- unauthorized repairs or modifications;
- normal cosmetic wear;
- loss or theft.

## Warranty Requests
Customers should provide:
- order number;
- product name;
- description of the issue;
- relevant photos or evidence when requested.

## Warranty vs Refund
A warranty request does not automatically create a refund.

The business may repair, replace, or otherwise resolve an eligible warranty issue according to the applicable manufacturer terms.

Refund requests remain subject to the Return and Refund Policies.
