# Khalid Electronics — Company FAQ

## About the Business
Khalid Electronics is a fictional Lagos-based online electronics retailer used for this AI customer-support project.

We sell smartphones, laptops, tablets, accessories, smart home devices, audio products, and wearables.

## Customer Support
Customer support is available Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM WAT.

## Payment Methods
Customers can pay using debit/credit cards, bank transfer, or approved online payment methods shown at checkout.

## Delivery
We deliver within Lagos and to other locations in Nigeria. Delivery fees and estimated delivery times depend on the destination and order value.

See the Shipping Policy for exact delivery fees and timeframes.

## Returns and Refunds
Customers can request returns within the stated return window when the order meets the Return Policy requirements.

Refund eligibility and processing are governed by the Refund Policy. Refunds require business-owner approval.

## Discounts
Standard promotional discounts may be applied according to the Discount Policy. Discounts above the permitted automatic limit require business-owner approval.

## Warranty
Warranty coverage depends on the product and manufacturer. Customers should check the product listing and Warranty Policy.

## Order Status
Logged-in customers can ask the support agent to check the status of their own orders.

For security, customers cannot use support to access another customer's order information.

## Changing an Order
Customers should contact support as soon as possible if they need to change an order. Changes are not guaranteed after an order has been shipped.

## Out-of-Scope Questions
If information cannot be verified from the business documents or authorized systems, the support agent should not invent an answer. It should explain that the information needs confirmation from a staff member.
